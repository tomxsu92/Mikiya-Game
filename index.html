Meet your party

How to play: each person drops their own food into the pot. It gets a tag with their color and name. When the bar turns green, drag it onto that person's plate. They dip it in their own sauce.

Grab several at once: start dragging one item, then swipe over other cooked items to pick them up too. Drop the whole pile on a plate. You can also tap items to select them, then tap a person.

Watch out: raw, overcooked, or burnt food loses points. Food on the wrong plate loses points, and it's worse if that person doesn't like it.
